Frequently Asked QuestionsPertanyaan Umum

Answers about opening hours, how to book, location, and treatments at Della Spa. Still have questions? Contact us directly via WhatsApp. Jawaban seputar jam buka, cara booking, lokasi, dan treatment Della Spa. Masih ada pertanyaan? Hubungi langsung lewat WhatsApp.

Do I need to make a reservation first?Apakah harus reservasi dulu?

To ensure your preferred time is available, we recommend contacting us via WhatsApp before you arrive. Untuk memastikan ketersediaan jam, sebaiknya hubungi kami lewat WhatsApp sebelum datang.

What time is Della Spa open?Jam berapa Della Spa buka?

Every day from 10.00–21.00 WITA, including weekends and national holidays. Setiap hari pukul 10.00–21.00 WITA, termasuk akhir pekan dan hari libur nasional.

How do I book?Bagaimana cara booking?

Send a message via WhatsApp to 0812-3610-9511, or use the Booking page to compose the message automatically for your chosen treatment. Kirim pesan lewat WhatsApp ke 0812-3610-9511, atau gunakan halaman Booking untuk menyusun pesan otomatis sesuai treatment yang diinginkan.

Where is Della Spa located?Di mana lokasi Della Spa?

Jl. Karma Kandara No.41, Ungasan, Kuta Selatan District, Badung Regency, Bali. In the Karma Kandara area, about 10 minutes from Melasti Beach. Jl. Karma Kandara No.41, Ungasan, Kecamatan Kuta Selatan, Kabupaten Badung, Bali. Berada di kawasan Karma Kandara, sekitar 10 menit dari Pantai Melasti.

How long is one treatment session?Berapa lama satu sesi treatment?

All body massages are one hour. Package 1 and Package 2 are two hours. Semua body massage berdurasi satu jam. Package 1 dan Package 2 berdurasi dua jam.

Is parking available?Apakah ada parkir?

Parking is available for motorbikes and cars near the location. Tersedia area parkir untuk motor dan mobil di sekitar lokasi.

Female or male therapist?Terapis wanita atau pria?

Please ask directly via WhatsApp when booking for your therapist preference. Silakan tanyakan langsung lewat WhatsApp saat booking untuk preferensi terapis.

What products are used for facials?Produk apa yang dipakai untuk facial?

Traditional Facial uses Della Spa's standard treatment products. For facials with Biokos products, Aloevera and Seaweed variants are available. Traditional Facial menggunakan produk perawatan standar Della Spa. Untuk facial dengan produk Biokos, tersedia varian Aloevera dan Seaweed.

Is it safe for pregnant guests?Apakah aman untuk ibu hamil?

For pregnancy or any specific medical condition, please let us know in advance via WhatsApp before booking. Untuk kondisi kehamilan atau kondisi medis tertentu, mohon informasikan lebih dulu lewat WhatsApp sebelum booking.

What payment methods are accepted?Metode pembayaran apa yang diterima?

Please ask about available payment methods via WhatsApp. Silakan tanyakan metode pembayaran yang tersedia lewat WhatsApp.

Does the staff speak English?Apakah staf bisa berbahasa Inggris?

For communication in English, please mention it via WhatsApp when booking. Untuk komunikasi dalam Bahasa Inggris, silakan sebutkan lewat WhatsApp saat booking.

What's the cheapest treatment?Berapa harga treatment paling murah?

Ear Candle starts from IDR 200,000. Body massage starts from IDR 300,000 per hour. See the full list on the Treatments & Prices page. Ear Candle mulai 200.000 rupiah. Untuk body massage, harga mulai 300.000 rupiah per jam. Daftar lengkap ada di halaman Treatment & Harga.

Can I book for more than one person?Apakah bisa booking untuk lebih dari satu orang?

Yes. Mention the number of guests when booking via WhatsApp or the Booking page, so we can prepare the right treatment room. Bisa. Sebutkan jumlah orang saat booking lewat WhatsApp atau di halaman Booking, supaya kami siapkan ruang treatment yang sesuai.

Still have questions?Masih ada pertanyaan?

Our team is ready to help via WhatsApp. Tim kami siap membantu lewat WhatsApp.
