## ⚡ Overview
Modern executive operations dashboard designed specifically for UPC Renewables to monitor wind & solar farm operations, renewable turbine metrics, SCADA telemetry, Microsoft 365 security governance, shift logs, and field incident remediation across APAC renewable asset facilities.

## 🚀 Features
1. **Executive Operational KPI & Telemetry**:
   - Power generation capacity (MW), turbine availability factor, grid curtailment, and environmental CO₂ offset.
   - Real-time SVG dynamic trendlines with high/low sparkline data indicators.
2. **Dynamic Renewable Energy Visuals**:
   - Integrated UPC Renewables official identity palette (Wind Turbine Teal #00A389 & Solar Gold #E5A93C).
   - Wind farm & solar operational photo galleries and technical infographics.
3. **M365 Intelligence & Attention Center**:
   - Collapsible priority briefing with contextual AI operational insights.
   - Kanban-style task & incident management fitted seamlessly into desktop viewports with zero horizontal scrollbars.
4. **Zero-Lag Offline Resilience & Accessibility**:
   - WCAG 2.2 AAA color contrast compliance.
   - Fully fluid full-width responsive architecture across mobile (320px) to ultra-wide (1920px+).
