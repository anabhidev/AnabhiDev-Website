Our services

Six ways the chef works in Bali. Each one is quoted per occasion — tell us the date, the location and the number of guests.

  • On Villa Dining

    The chef cooks and serves in your villa. Menu agreed in advance, kitchen left clean.

  • Private BBQ

    Grill-led menus built around the day's catch, meats and house-made sauces.

  • Catering

    Larger groups — villa parties, corporate gatherings, celebrations and weddings.

  • Cooking Class

    Hands-on sessions in your own kitchen, working through a menu you get to keep.

  • Special Events

    Proposals, anniversaries, birthdays and dinners that need the table to feel like the occasion.

  • Wine & Mixology

    Curated wine pairings and bespoke cocktails to elevate your private dining experience.
