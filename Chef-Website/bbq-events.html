The centrepiece

Balinese babi guling

A whole suckling pig, turned over an open fire and served the traditional way. Cooked on site, carved at your table.

  • What is served

    Whole babi guling, sayur urab, jukut ares, steamed rice, and black rice pudding with mango and coconut milk.

  • Group size

    Built for 10 to 13 guests. Larger groups quoted on request.

  • From

    IDR 4,750,000 per package. All ingredients and preparation included — no separate chef fee.

How it works

From message to table

  • 01

    Tell us the occasion

    Date, villa, number of guests, and anything the group cannot eat.

  • 02

    Agree the menu

    The chef replies on WhatsApp with a menu direction and a quote.

  • 03

    We bring everything

    Ingredients, equipment and the fire. Nothing needed from the villa.

  • 04

    Cooked on site

    Roasting starts hours ahead so it is ready when your guests are.

  • 05

    Served your way

    Carved at the table, or plated and paced as courses.

  • 06

    Kitchen left clean

    The work area is cleared as if no one had cooked there.
