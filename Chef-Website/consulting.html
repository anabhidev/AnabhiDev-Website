Methodology

Our consulting approach

A structured four-phase process ensuring measurable improvements from audit to ongoing success.

  • 01 Discovery & Audit

    In-depth assessment of your current culinary operations, guest feedback, margins, and market positioning.

  • 02 Strategy & Concept

    Developing the concept brief, menu architecture, cost modeling, and operational roadmap.

  • 03 Implementation & Training

    Hands-on kitchen execution, recipe tastings, staff training, and launch preparation.

  • 04 Optimization & Review

    Post-launch auditing, yield adjustments, and ongoing advisory to sustain culinary excellence.
