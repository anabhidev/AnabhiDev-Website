Case studies

What changed after I built it.

Internal platforms built for a hospitality group. No public links — but these are the ones with numbers behind them.

01

Learning Management System

2025 — 2026

Problem

Training 110+ staff across departments meant paper checklists, WhatsApp reminders, and no reliable way to prove who had actually completed what.

Built

A full LMS from scratch — course delivery, a quiz engine with anti-cheating measures, role-based access, and automatic certificates. An AI layer on top writes the daily and monthly summaries management actually reads.

Result

110+ staff on the platform. Training status went from a question nobody could answer to a dashboard anyone can open.

02

Knowledge-Based AI Assistant

2025 — 2026

Problem

The same operational questions reached the IT desk every week — where a policy lives, how a system works, what the procedure is for an edge case.

Built

A conversational assistant grounded in the company's own documentation, so answers come from internal reality rather than generic model knowledge.

Result

Routine lookups now resolve without a human in the loop. In testing with the operations team.

IT Intelligence Dashboard

2025 — 2026

Problem

Infrastructure status lived in five different tools. Nobody outside IT could see it, and inside IT it took a morning to assemble.

Built

One operational dashboard consolidating network, systems and service health — written for a non-technical reader as much as for the IT team.

Result

In daily use. Status reporting moved from reactive to at-a-glance.

04

Jagadita Consulting — Website Rebuild

2026

Problem

A hospitality consulting firm's site was running on WordPress — slow to load, heavy to maintain, and no longer matching the quality of the advice it was selling.

Built

A full redesign and rebuild as a static site — new UI, hand-written HTML/CSS/JS, deployed on Cloudflare. Same content, structured for speed instead of a database.

Result

Live at jagadita.com. A faster, lighter site with no CMS overhead — one less moving part for the client to maintain.
