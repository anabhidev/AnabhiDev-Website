01

About

I've spent eleven years as the person a hotel calls when the PMS stops talking to the POS at 11pm on New Year's Eve. Pre-openings, system migrations, network builds, vendor negotiations — six properties across Bali, including a 76-villa resort I brought online from bare walls.

In 2025 I started building the software instead of just buying it. What began as small automations became Anabhi Dev — a working catalogue of 59 projects, 37 of them deployed and running. A learning management system with an AI reporting layer. A knowledge-based chatbot for restaurant operations. Dashboards that turn IT noise into a morning summary someone actually reads.

That combination is the whole point. I know why a training module fails on the floor, because I've watched it fail. I know what a GM will actually open on their phone. Domain knowledge is the hard part; the code is how I express it.

Currently IT Manager at Miss Fish Bali, and open to remote roles and freelance projects anywhere.
