Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about applying to Miss Fish Bali.

Do I need prior Japanese restaurant experience to apply? +
While previous Japanese cuisine or sushi bar experience is an advantage for senior roles, for many of our positions passion, knife discipline, cleanliness, and willingness to learn are far more important. We provide intensive masterclasses for new team members.
Is English fluency required? +
For guest-facing roles (Hostess, Floor Server, Bartender, Sommelier), conversational to fluent English is required as we serve an international clientele. For kitchen and back-of-house roles, basic hospitality English is sufficient, as daily culinary coaching is conducted in both Indonesian and English.
What should I prepare for the kitchen or bar trial? +
For kitchen trials, bring your favorite chef knives if you prefer, wear comfortable non-slip kitchen shoes, and be prepared to demonstrate fish preparation, knife precision, and station cleanliness. For bar trials, you will be invited to craft classic cocktails and demonstrate guest charm.
How does the ClickUp application form work? +
Our application form creates a secure candidate record directly in our ClickUp Recruitment System of Record. Your private details and CV are only accessible by authorized Miss Fish HR personnel.
