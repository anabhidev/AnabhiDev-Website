Step 1 of 8

Your First Login

Your IT or HR team will give you an Employee ID and a temporary password. Use these to log in for the first time.

1

Open the LMS link

Use the link shared by IT/HR — it works on both computer and phone browsers.

2

Enter your Employee ID and password

Employee ID format looks like MF0042. Type it exactly as given to you.

3

Tap the Sign In button

You'll land directly on your Dashboard.

About your password

The password IT/HR gave you is temporary. If you'd like to change it, go to your Profile menu (top right) → Change Password. Changing it is optional for now, not required to continue.

Trouble logging in?

Double-check your Employee ID has no extra spaces, and that Caps Lock isn't accidentally on for your password. Still stuck? See the Need Help page in this manual.

Your Dashboard

This is the first screen you see after Login. It shows a quick summary and the courses assigned to you.

👋

Welcome message

Shows your name and department at the top.

📚

My Courses

How many courses assigned to you, and how many you've finished.

🏆

Certificates

Certificates you've earned so far.

Finding your course

1

Tap the My Courses menu

On phone, this is usually in the bottom navigation bar. On computer, it's in the left sidebar.

2

You'll see a list of courses

Only courses assigned to you (or to your department) appear here — you won't see courses meant for other departments.

3

Tap a course card to open it

Each card shows the course title and how much progress you've made.

Deadline reminder

If a course is close to or past its deadline, a reminder banner appears automatically at the top of your Dashboard. You don't need to check manually.

Step 3 of 8

Opening a Course

A course is made of one or more modules — video, PDF, or quiz. You go through them in order, from top to bottom.

1

You'll see a list of modules

Each one shows its type (Video / PDF / Quiz) and whether it's already done.

2

Tap the first module to start

The first module is always open. The rest are locked until you finish the one before it.

3

Finish one, unlock the next

As soon as one module is marked complete, the next one unlocks automatically.

Why can't I skip ahead?

This is intentional — modules are locked in order on purpose, so training material is followed step by step, not skipped around.

Step 4 of 8

Watching a Video

Video modules track how much you actually watch — not just whether you opened it.

RuleWhat it means
Must watch ~95%You need to watch almost the whole video before it's marked complete. Jumping to the end won't work.
No speeding upPlayback speed is locked at normal speed — there's no 1.5x or 2x option.
Switching tabs pauses itIf you switch to another app or browser tab, the video automatically pauses. Come back and tap Play to continue.
Resumes where you left offClose the video and come back later — it picks up right where you stopped, not from the beginning.
Slow internet?

If the video keeps buffering, try switching from mobile data to WiFi, or ask IT/HR for a quiet spot with better signal during the trial.

Step 5 of 8

Reading a PDF

PDF modules are used for SOPs, guides, and written materials. You read them right inside the LMS — no need to download anything.

Basic controls

🔍

Zoom

Pinch to zoom on phone, or use the zoom buttons on computer. Text stays sharp even zoomed in.

📖

1 page / 2 pages

Switch between single-page view and a two-page spread (like a real book).

➡️

Flip pages

Swipe, or tap the arrow buttons, to turn to the next page.

How a PDF module gets marked complete

You need to go through every page to the end — not just open the file. Opening it and closing it right away won't complete the module.

Listening instead of reading

Some PDFs have an Audio button (usually shown in red) — tap it to have the document read out loud to you.

1

Tap the red Audio button

It will start reading from the beginning of the document, page by page.

2

Pages turn automatically

You don't need to flip pages yourself while listening — it follows along and turns the page for you.

3

You can't skip ahead in the audio

Same idea as video — the reading speed is fixed, and you can't jump forward. You can always tap the Text button to read along on screen instead.

Bilingual SOP — new feature

Some SOPs are written in two columns — English on the left, Indonesian on the right. For these documents, the audio bar shows an EN / ID toggle so you can choose which language to listen to. Switching language continues from the same part of the document — it doesn't restart from page 1.

Toggle not showing up?

That's normal for most PDFs — the EN/ID toggle only appears for SOPs written in that specific two-column format. Regular PDFs still have Audio, just without the language choice.

Step 6 of 8

Taking a Quiz

A quiz is usually the last module in a course. You need to pass it to complete the course and get your certificate (if the course has one).

RuleWhat it means
Questions are randomEach attempt pulls a random set of questions from a larger question bank — a retry won't show you the exact same questions.
1.5 minutes per questionThe timer is based on how many questions you get — more questions means more time. It submits automatically when time runs out.
Limited attemptsYou get a set number of tries (ask your HoD/HR if unsure how many for this course). Once you run out, you'll see a clear message that the limit is reached.
Minimum score to passEach course sets its own passing score. You'll see your score and pass/fail result right after submitting.
Before you start

Once the timer starts, it keeps running even if you close the tab or lose signal — so make sure you're ready and have a stable connection before tapping Start.

Didn't pass?

That's what retry attempts are for. Review the module content again before your next try — the explanation for each answer often shows after you submit.

Step 7 of 8

Your Certificate

If a course offers a certificate, you'll receive it automatically the moment you pass the quiz — no need to request it from anyone.

1

Pass the quiz

Your score needs to meet the course's minimum passing score.

2

Certificate appears right away

It's generated instantly — go to the Certificates menu to see it.

3

View or download it

Your name, score, and the course title are filled in automatically.

Certificates expire

Most certificates are valid for a set period (often 1 year). After that, the certificate is marked expired — it's not deleted, but it's no longer considered current. If a course needs regular re-certification, you'll need to retake it once your certificate expires.

Step 8 of 8

Need Help?

This is a trial period — it's completely normal to run into questions or something that doesn't work as expected. Here's who to reach out to.

👥

Ask HR

Best for: which courses you're assigned, deadlines, or how a certificate is used for your role.

💻

Ask IT

Best for: can't log in, video/PDF won't load, the app feels stuck or broken.

Before you reach out

If something looks wrong, try closing and reopening the page first — that solves most small glitches. If it still doesn't work, tell HR/IT exactly what you were doing when it happened (which course, which module) — that helps them fix it faster.

This is a trial, not the final system

A few things may still be rough around the edges — that's exactly what this trial is for. Your feedback helps make the real rollout smoother for everyone.
