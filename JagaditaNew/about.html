Our Philosophy

Jagad · Hita · Well-being

The name Jagadita is rooted in Nusantara philosophy — Jagad meaning world, and Hita meaning well-being. Together, they reflect our belief that great hospitality harmonizes people, place, and purpose.

We connect local wisdom with modern strategy and technology to shape future-ready hospitality businesses — where operational excellence and authentic guest experiences coexist.

Founded on 23 March 2026 in Bali, Jagadita brings together 65 years of combined expertise across hospitality strategy, operations, and technology to serve Hotels, Resorts, Villas, and F&B businesses across Indonesia and beyond.

The People Behind Jagadita

Meet Our Founders

Viveka Nanda
COO & Co-Founder

Viveka is an accomplished hospitality executive with proven expertise in hotel, resort and serviced apartment operations, as well as asset development, corporate governance, and hospitality investment strategy. He has held executive and board-level positions with leading hospitality groups including Accor Hotels, Marriott Autograph Collection, Choice Hotels, New Nordic Group, and Sinar Mas Land. His expertise includes hotel openings, rebranding, operational leadership, revenue optimization, financial management, and stakeholder relations. Throughout his career, Viveka has successfully led numerous flagship hospitality projects and transformations across Indonesia. A graduate of Université de Paris - Sorbonne, he combines academic refinement with a distinguished hospitality leadership experience.

Satria Wibawa
CEO & Co-Founder

Anak Agung Satria Wibawa, S.Par is the driving force behind Miss Fish Bali, leading the venue from pre-opening in 2022 into a standout lifestyle destination. With over 15 years of hospitality experience across Jakarta and Bali — including roles with BIKO Group, Wyndham Dreamland Resort, and Manarai Beach House — he combines strong operational expertise with global standards shaped from early cruise-ship service. Backed by formal education in Hotel Management and Tourism (S.Par; Master's 2024), he is known for building high-performing, cross-functional teams and driving seamless operations, consistent service recovery, and a refined guest journey from elevated dining to high-energy nightlife.

Agus Wibawa
CTO & Co-Founder

Agus Wibawa is an experienced IT professional with over two decades in technology and 11 years specialising in hospitality operations, with a proven track record in hotel pre-openings, system migrations, and managing PMS, infrastructure, and operations. He holds a Master's degree in IT from Institut Teknologi Bandung and a Business Administration degree, combining technical expertise with strategic insight to optimize operations, enhance guest experiences, and deliver scalable, future-ready solutions that drive sustainable growth. He also provides tailored Hospitality IT consulting, aligning technology with business goals, improving system integration, and maximizing ROI with a hands-on, results-driven approach.

Our Journey

The Road to Jagadita

1989

A Career Rooted in Hospitality

Viveka Nanda begins his hospitality journey — the start of a 37-year career spanning hotel openings, rebranding, operational leadership, and executive roles with Accor Hotels, Marriott Autograph Collection, Choice Hotels, and Sinar Mas Land across Indonesia.

2002

Technology Roots Begin

Agus Wibawa joins the early digital community Forumponsel.com — the beginning of a technology journey that would eventually bridge IT expertise with hospitality operations, system integrations, and hotel pre-openings.

2009

Operations & Academic Excellence

Satria Wibawa begins his hospitality career — 17 years across F&B, resort operations, and lifestyle venues in Bali and Jakarta. Simultaneously, Agus Wibawa completes his Master of Information Systems at Institut Teknologi Bandung, combining technical depth with strategic insight.

2026

Jagadita Is Born

On 23 March 2026, three hospitality professionals unite under one vision — to help hospitality brands operate smarter, serve better, and earn more. Jagadita Hospitality Consulting is officially founded in Bali, Indonesia.
