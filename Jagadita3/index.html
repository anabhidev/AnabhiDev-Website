About Us

Jagadita Hospitality Consulting helps hospitality brands build sustainable, authentic businesses through strategy, operations, and technology-driven approaches.

Inspired by Nusantara philosophy — Jagad (world) and Hita (well-being) — we connect local wisdom with modern strategy and technology to shape future-ready hospitality businesses.

Our services span: Hospitality Strategy, concept development, guest journey, branding, operational excellence, SOP, service standards, and performance improvement.
